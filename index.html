Flip 7
With a Vengeance

Live scoreboard

The goal

Each round, collect 7 different number cards (1 to 13) without flipping a duplicate. First to reach the target score at the end of a round wins the game.

The cards

On your turn

  • Flip a card from the deck onto your row, or Stay and bank what you have.
  • Flip a duplicate number and you bust: 0 points this round.
  • Collect 7 unique numbers = Flip 7! The round ends immediately and you get a +15 bonus.
  • Action cards (such as Freeze, Flip Three, Second Chance) are resolved as soon as they are flipped. Check your physical rulebook for the full Vengeance action card list.

Round score

  1. Add all number cards (Lucky 13 = 13, The Zero = 0).
  2. If you hold the ÷2 power card, halve it and round up.
  3. Subtract the minus power cards. The score cannot go below 0.
  4. If you flipped 7 cards, add 15.
  5. Bust = 0 points.
  6. The Zero: you need 7 cards including the Zero itself (other power cards do not count), otherwise your points are not counted.

End of game

  • Ends when at least one player has reached the target (default 200) at the end of a round.
  • Highest total wins.
  • If the top score is tied, play one more round.

Chip twist

House rule. Chips are score multipliers, used only in Chip mode. Normal mode is the same game without chips.

  • One chip type, each worth 1.
  • Everyone starts round 1 with 5 chips and receives 2 more when each round ends. Chips carry over.
  • Before flipping, bet any number of chips you hold. Betting 0 counts as x1.
  • After normal scoring (including the +15 bonus), the round score is multiplied by the chips bet.
  • If you bust: you score 0 and lose the chips you bet. Otherwise you keep them.
  • Late joiners get 5 + 2 x rounds already played chips, the same as a player who never lost chips.

Worked example

45 -4 -8 x3
(45 - 4 - 8) = 33
33 x 3 = 99 points

Number cards 45, power cards -4 and -8, bet 3 chips. Had this player busted, they would score 0 and lose the 3 chips.
